# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in our community is respected and valued.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and inclusive of differing opinions and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Being patient and helpful to newcomers

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Offensive comments related to gender, sexual orientation, disability, race, or religion
- Deliberate intimidation or threats
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, flaming, or similar disruptive behavior
- Publishing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

- Warning or temporary suspension from the repository
- Permanent ban from the community

To report violations, please contact [@coder3r](https://github.com/coder3r).
